About Ivy

Developer and researcher building systems for Philippine public institutions.

I design and build systems that solve real operational problems in public institutions — inventory, personnel, records, and safety — using tools that schools and offices can actually run and maintain.

My work brings together a background in information technology and computer science with quantitative training in financial engineering, and it is now pointed toward public administration: systems that measure, allocate, and account for public resources transparently.
